# ImageGen visual storytelling plan

## Guardrails

The generated images in this project are branded editorial illustrations. They are not photographs of Central Auto Repair, its owners, staff, customers, storefront, or equipment.

- Never use generated imagery as a customer testimonial or review.
- Never caption generated imagery as the actual shop, team, owner, father, son, or equipment.
- Keep the About illustration visibly labeled as editorial artwork.
- Replace or supplement these images with permission-cleared exterior, team, bay, and TPMS-tool photography when available.
- Real reviews, certifications, warranty terms, affiliations, and operating claims must come from approved business evidence.

## Service-guide visual system

The complete service-guide set uses eleven responsive editorial illustrations generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Each asset teaches one system relationship without representing a diagnosis, a completed repair, or the actual shop. The same image is shared by its English and Spanish guide so localization does not create competing visual claims. Every final file is normalized to an 8:5 crop: 768 × 480 for mobile and 1280 × 800 for larger screens.

Shared art direction:

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: responsive website service-guide editorial illustration
Scene/backdrop: warm off-white paper field with subtle recycled-fiber grain
Style/medium: premium two-color linocut and screenprint editorial illustration, hand-inked edges, tactile halftone, confident negative space, sophisticated independent auto-shop identity
Composition/framing: wide composition with large crop-safe forms and no tiny technical detail
Color palette: charcoal black and safety orange on warm off-white, with only a very small acid-yellow highlight
Constraints: no words, letters, numbers, labels, logos, trademarks, people, faces, storefronts, license plates, fake photography, watermarks, gradients, glossy 3D, brand-specific parts, or unsafe behavior; mechanically plausible simplified objects; accessible contrast; clean margins for responsive cropping
```

Asset-specific requests:

- Maintenance and oil: a motor-oil droplet connected to an odometer dial, pleated filter, and open owner's-manual silhouette, presenting maintenance as a rhythm rather than a universal interval.
- Brakes: a brake pedal connected to a disc rotor and caliper, showing pedal input becoming stopping force without presenting a diagnosis.
- Tires and wheels: one concentric tire, wheel, and hub meeting the road at a believable contact patch, with motion converging at the point where the vehicle meets the road. Avoid damage, skidding, alignment targets, or a balance diagnosis.
- Engine diagnostics: a dashboard warning clue passing through a magnifying lens and resolving into several possible system forms, reinforcing that a warning light requires testing.
- Battery and electrical: a battery, starter gear, alternator pulley, and headlamp arranged as a closed start-and-charge loop.
- Heating and air conditioning: one connected cabin-air path beginning at the blower, passing through temperature-control sections and a movable door, then branching toward windshield, dashboard, and floor vents. Airflow must remain inside coherent ducts.
- Cooling and overheating: an engine, pump, coolant path, radiator, and airflow arranged as a heat-management loop, with no leaks, steam, or unsafe imagery.
- Steering and suspension: a single front-corner cutaway showing a tire following a modest road bump while a connected steering rack and tie rod control direction and the spring and damper control vertical movement.
- Belts and hoses: one engine supports two visibly separate paths—a continuous serpentine belt carrying rotation across pulleys and enclosed hoses carrying coolant to and from a heat exchanger. The paths must never merge.
- Transmission and drivetrain: one illustrative longitudinal power path flows from a transmission through a driveshaft and differential, then branches through jointed shafts to the driven wheels. It is presented as one possible layout, not a universal vehicle diagram.
- Exhaust and emissions: a side cutaway keeps one continuous exhaust path below the passenger floor, passing from the engine through connected treatment and sound-control sections before exiting behind the cabin. No gas enters the passenger compartment.

These illustrations should use meaningful localized alt text because they explain the adjacent system story. The layout and copy must still make sense when images are unavailable.
