The people behind the work

Experience passed forward.

The son owns and leads Central Auto Repair. Beside him is his father, who brings decades of hands-on auto repair experience to their work together.

It is a father-and-son story, but it is also a working partnership. One generation contributes years spent solving problems in the shop. The next leads Central today while working alongside that experience.

A shop you can call directly

When a warning light appears, a brake starts making noise, or the car simply feels different, you do not need to know the repair name. Call Central, describe what you notice, and ask whether the shop is the right place to begin.

Rooted on Route 27

Central Auto Repair serves drivers from its Edison location at 82 Lincoln Highway. The shop is open Monday through Saturday from 9 AM to 7 PM and is closed Sunday.
