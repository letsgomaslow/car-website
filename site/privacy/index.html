Privacy.

This page describes how the current public website handles information.

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Information collection

The current website does not include an online appointment or contact form, user accounts, advertising trackers, or site analytics. Calling the shop uses your phone provider, and Central Auto Repair may receive the phone information normally provided with a call.

External services

The website loads font files from Google Fonts. It also links to Google Maps for directions. When you use these resources, the external provider may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details, and the page or link requested under its own privacy terms.

Cookies

The current site does not intentionally set first-party cookies. External resources may operate under their own policies.

Future changes

If online forms, analytics, advertising, or other data-collection tools are added, this notice should be updated before those features are enabled.
