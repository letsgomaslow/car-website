What the system does

Pressure data, translated.

A tire pressure monitoring system uses sensors to alert the driver when the system detects a pressure concern or cannot read a sensor correctly. Programming connects compatible sensor information to the vehicle.

Common questions

Before you call.

These answers explain the starting point. The specific service depends on the vehicle and sensor.

Does a tire pressure light always mean a bad sensor?

No. The light can indicate low tire pressure, a temperature-related pressure change, a sensor communication issue, or another TPMS concern. The vehicle needs to be checked before the cause is assumed.

Can every replacement sensor be programmed?

Sensor and vehicle compatibility varies. Call with the year, make, model, and sensor information so Central can confirm whether the requested programming is appropriate.

What is a TPMS relearn?

A relearn is the process a vehicle uses to recognize sensor positions or identifiers after certain tire or sensor work. The required procedure depends on the vehicle.

Can I keep driving with the light on?

Check the tires safely and follow the vehicle manufacturer’s guidance. If a tire is visibly low, damaged, or the vehicle feels unsafe, stop driving and seek assistance.
